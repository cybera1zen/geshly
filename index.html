How it works

2

Open a problem and see the real post or review it came from.

3

Say "me too"

Upvote problems you share. The most felt ones rise to the top.

4

Build

Save the best ones and start making a solution people want.

Solo builders, indie hackers and small teams who have the skills but no idea. Also anyone who wants to share a problem they face and see if others feel it too.
